Privacy Policy

Bible Blitz, published by Alpine Squad · Last updated: May 14, 2026

Bible Blitz ("the App", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly what information the App handles and how.

Summary

Bible Blitz does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. All information you enter or generate while using the App stays on your device.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework. None of it leaves your device:

  • An optional display name you enter during onboarding (used only to personalize greetings in the App)
  • Quiz history, scores, XP, level, and streak progress
  • App preferences (Bible translation, theme, sound, haptics, daily reminder time)
  • Achievement unlock status and per-question learning progress (spaced repetition)

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not transmit your name, email, location, or any personal identifier to any server
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services
  • We do not use advertising frameworks or share data with advertisers
  • We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, or similar technologies
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time premium purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase process.

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No notification data is sent to any server controlled by us.

Children's Privacy

Bible Blitz is suitable for users of all ages. Because the App stores everything locally and collects no personal information, no information about any user — including children under 13 — is ever transmitted or retained off-device.

Data Deletion

All App data can be deleted by uninstalling Bible Blitz from your device. Because nothing is stored externally, uninstalling permanently removes all associated data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. The current version is always available at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to support@alpinesquad.com or opened as an issue on our GitHub repository.
