Common questions

  • Restoring a purchase: open Settings inside the App and tap "Restore Purchases".
  • Fresh predictions: the App downloads new current predictions automatically when it has an internet connection. Saved predictions keep working offline.
  • Something looks off on the water: use "Report a problem" in the App's Settings — a note with the place and time helps us improve the model blend.
  • Safety: Current Charts shows model predictions, not measurements. Never use it as your only source for navigation decisions.
