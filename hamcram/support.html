Common questions

  • Which exams are covered? Technician (valid through June 2030) and General (valid through June 2027), using the official NCVEC question pools. Amateur Extra is coming in a future update.
  • Are the questions the real ones? Yes — the complete, current FCC question pools, parsed from the official NCVEC source, including figures.
  • Your study data: stored on your device (and in your private iCloud, if signed in). We never see it.
  • Practice exams mirror the real 35-question VE test, scored the same way (pass at 26).
