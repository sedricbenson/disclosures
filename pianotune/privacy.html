Privacy Policy

Pianotune, published by Alpine Squad · Last updated: May 23, 2026

Pianotune ("the App", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly what information the App handles and how.

Summary

Pianotune does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. All audio processing and any data the App generates stays on your device.

Microphone Access

Pianotune requires microphone access to perform real-time pitch detection — this is the core function of a tuning app. Audio captured from the microphone is processed entirely on-device, in memory, for the sole purpose of detecting pitch. Microphone audio is never recorded or transmitted off your device, with one optional exception:

  • If you explicitly enable "Record session audio" in Settings, the App will write WAV files of your tuning sessions to the App's local sandbox on your device. These files are stored locally only — they are never uploaded to any server, never shared with us, and never shared with any third party. You can delete them at any time from within the App or by uninstalling.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not transmit your name, email, location, or any personal identifier to any server
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services
  • We do not use advertising frameworks or share data with advertisers
  • We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, or similar technologies
  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect any personally identifying information
  • We do not request access to your contacts, photos, or camera

Cloud Sync

Pianotune v1.0 does not sync any data to iCloud or any other cloud service. CloudKit-backed sync is on the roadmap for a future release; if and when it is added, this policy will be updated to reflect exactly what is synced and how.

In-App Purchases

The initial release of Pianotune is free and contains no in-app purchases.

Diagnostic Data

The App uses Apple's MetricKit framework to capture crash reports and performance diagnostic payloads. These payloads are stored only in the App's local Documents directory on your device. Nothing leaves the device — they are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Pianotune is not directed at children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information from any user, no information about any user — including children — is ever transmitted or retained off-device.

Data Deletion

All App data can be deleted by uninstalling Pianotune from your device. Because nothing is stored externally, uninstalling permanently removes all associated data, including any recorded session audio and diagnostic payloads.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. The current version is always available at this URL.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to support@alpinesquad.com.
