Common questions

  • How do I play? Guess any word from the category. Each guess tells you how many words away you are in A-to-Z order and which direction to look. Trap the word between two guesses and squeeze the bracket shut before your guesses run out. A full tutorial is under Settings → How to Play.
  • Restoring a purchase: open Settings inside the App and tap "Restore Purchases". This restores the "Unlock Everything" purchase; consumable hint packs are added to your balance at purchase and cannot be restored.
  • Hints: you earn a hint token every win — two for beating par — and can buy more from the lightbulb button in a game.
  • Your progress: all progress is stored on your device. Uninstalling the App removes it permanently.
  • The daily puzzle is always free and is the same word for everyone each day. Its category is also free to play all day.
